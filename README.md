## About
 
I build and operate cloud platforms on AWS and Azure. Terraform provisions the environment, GitHub Actions and Argo CD ship to it, and Datadog tells me when it breaks before a customer does. Three years of that so far, across consulting and product teams.
 
I've carried a pager for customer-facing production systems and spent a fair amount of time on the other side of an incident bridge deployment history, Kubernetes events, ingress behaviour, while someone else digs through the application. Most of what I care about is downstream of that: least privilege by default, runbooks someone can actually follow at 3am, and post-incident reviews that change something.
 
Currently finishing an M.Sc. in International Software Systems Science at the University of Bamberg.
